## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a **Modern Classic Bookstore** aesthetic—evoking the quiet authority of an archival library fused with the frictionless precision of contemporary e-commerce.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Curated & Intellectual:** Prioritizes editorial legibility, high typographic craft, and intellectual quietude over aggressive, loud sales patterns.
- **Trustworthy & Enduring:** Anchored by structured deep navy and balanced margins that evoke prestige, dependability, and academic thoroughness.
- **Refined Hospitality:** Welcoming warm-paper undertones that eliminate eye strain during prolonged reading and browsing sessions.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
The visual direction fuses **Minimalist Editorial** with **Tactile Subtlety**:
- Crisp layout architecture featuring delicate 1px perimeter rules.
- Atmospheric card lift using low-opacity deep navy ambient drop shadows.
- Generous micro-spacing around covers, author credits, and critical purchasing metadata to ensure book covers remain the primary focal points.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional gravitas with vibrant editorial accents on clean, paper-tinted surfaces.

### Primary & Brand Hierarchy
- **Primary Navy (`#0F2942` / Hover: `#1E3A5F`):** Used for primary conversion triggers, main headers, global navigation bars, and primary structural typography.
- **Deep Teal (`#0D9488` / Accent: `#14B8A6`):** Applied to active navigation indicators, key reading recommendations, category tags, and secondary action items.
- **Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`):** Reserved exclusively for rating stars, flash deal callouts, and curated best-seller badges.

### Surface & Border Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF9F6` (Warm Book White) for the root background; `#FFFFFF` for product card surfaces, reading sheets, and checkout modules.
- **Subtle Surface:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for secondary wells, filter panels, and metadata chips.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Hairline `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for sharp editorial structure without visual weight.

### Semantic Status Badges
Status indicators use soft-tinted backgrounds with crisp text labels:
- **Pending Confirmation (Chờ xác nhận):** `#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` text (Amber).
- **Confirmed (Đã xác nhận):** `#E2E8F0` background with `#334155` text (Slate Navy).
- **In Transit (Đang giao):** `#E0F2FE` background with `#0369A1` text (Sky Blue).
- **Completed (Hoàn thành):** `#D1FAE5` background with `#047857` text (Emerald).
- **Cancelled (Đã hủy):** `#FFE4E6` background with `#BE123C` text (Rose).

## Typography

Typographic scale is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, marrying geometric clarity with sculpted, humanist letterforms that excel in dense e-commerce cataloging.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for curated collection titles, hero banners, and catalog section headers. Negative letter-spacing is applied to maintain visual density.
- **Product Titles:** Set in `title-sm` with a strict 2-line clamp to ensure consistent card heights across book listing grids.
- **Metadata (Authors & Publishers):** Styled using `body-sm` in `#64748B` (Slate 500) to create clear visual separation beneath book titles.
- **Financial Figures (Prices):** Set in bold variants (`price-lg`, `price-md`) in `#0F2942` for current prices, paired with strikethrough original prices in `body-sm` (`#94A3B8`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container constraints to maintain optimal scanning rhythms.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1360px`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer `margin: 2rem`. Product grids render 4 to 5 books per row.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1.25rem`, outer `margin: 1.5rem`. Product grids adapt to 3 books per row.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter: 1rem`, outer `margin: 1rem`. Product displays convert to a 2-column card grid or horizontal scrolling shelves.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Component Tightness (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Gaps between rating stars, badges, price deltas, and metadata lines.
- **Container Insets (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Internal padding for book cards, filter panels, review containers, and checkout summaries.
- **Section Breaks (`space-xl`):** Vertical clearance between distinct editorial carousels and promotional sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered structural tones reinforced by soft, tinted ambient shadows that reference book jackets on clean shelving.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Recessed):** Background canvas (`#FAF9F6`) and neutral form inputs. Outlined with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Card Resting):** Product cards, review items, and filter panels. Elevated by:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04);` bordered by `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards):** Triggered when hovering over book cards:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.03);` with a translateY(-2px) transition.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Flyouts):** Dropdown menus, mini-cart overlays, and autocomplete panels:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04);`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Quick View):** Quick-view dialogs and checkout popups:
  `box-shadow: 0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.22);` paired with a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)` over `#0F2942` at 40% opacity).

## Shapes

The system implements a **Soft (Level 1)** curvature discipline. This retains the crisp geometry of physical books while softening interactive edges to maintain an accessible, modern aesthetic.

### Radius Assignments
- **Base Components (`rounded`: 0.25rem / 4px):** Checkboxes, form text inputs, small status badges, and book cover container corners.
- **Cards & Panels (`rounded-lg`: 0.5rem / 8px):** Book cards, order summary surfaces, dialogue wrappers, and banners.
- **Overlays (`rounded-xl`: 0.75rem / 12px):** Global modals and multi-step checkout drawers.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter selection tags, search bars, and quantity increment steppers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F2942`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `rounded`, font `label-md`. Hover state transitions to `#1E3A5F`. Focused with a 2px outline in `#0D9488` with a 2px offset.
- **Secondary (Teal Action):** Background `#0D9488`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover state switches to `#14B8A6`. Used for "Add to Cart" and primary browsing incentives.
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1px border `#E2E8F0`, background transparent, text `#0F2942`. Hover state applies `#F8FAFC`.

### Book Cards
- **Structure:** White card background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded-lg` wrapper with `space-md` internal padding.
- **Image Frame:** Fixed aspect ratio `2:3` for book covers, framed with a subtle inner border (`rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`) and `rounded` corners to reflect physical book trimming.
- **Content Flow:**
  1. Category / Format tag (e.g., "Bìa Cứng") in `label-sm`.
  2. Book Title in `title-sm` with a 2-line clamp.
  3. Author & Publisher in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).
  4. Review Row: Amber star rating icons (`#F59E0B`), numerical score, and total review count in parentheses.
  5. Price Group: Primary highlighted sale price paired with a muted strikethrough retail price.

### Status Badges
- Displayed with an inline-flex layout, `rounded-full` or `rounded` profile, `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Built with high-contrast text over 10-15% tinted tint backgrounds according to the semantic rules defined in the Colors section.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Inset padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `body-md`.
- Focus state: Border color transitions to `#0D9488` with a surrounding `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)` ring.
- Global Search includes an icon affix, quick search suggestions, and a pill-shaped exterior.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: 18x18px, `rounded` (3px), border `#94A3B8`. When checked: Background `#0D9488` with a crisp white check icon.
- Radio Buttons: 18x18px circular, checked state features a `#0F2942` perimeter surrounding an inner dot in `#0D9488`.

### Chips & Taxonomy Filters
- Unselected: Background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#475569`.
- Selected: Background `#0F2942`, border `1px solid #0F2942`, text `#FFFFFF`. Includes a teal accent mark for current counts.
